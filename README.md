# Beginner's Guide Blueprint

**Status:** Initialization | **Phase:** P0 | **Started:** 2026-05-26

## Project Identity

A blueprint/structured plan for creating a comprehensive beginner's guide to quantum computing and quantum technologies, drawing on a curated collection of source materials including a 2025 Springer textbook and key research papers.

## Source Materials (35 PDFs)

### Primary Source — Textbook
- **"Quantum Technologies: Trends and Implications for Cyber Defense"** (Springer, 2025, ISBN 978-3-031-90727-2)
  - 22 chapters covering superconducting qubits, trapped ions, neutral atoms, semiconductor spins, error correction, quantum optimization, quantum machine learning, and cyber defense implications
  - Editors: Julian Jang-Jaccard, Philippe Caroff, Evan Blezinger, Valentin Mulder, Alain Mermoud, Vincent Lenders

## Goal

To produce a well-structured beginner's guide/blueprint for quantum computing — what topics to cover, in what order, at what depth — grounded in these authoritative source materials.

## Constraints

- All quantitative claims must be code-verified
- Math must use LaTeX/MathJax formatting
- No fabricated citations or data
- Target audience: educated beginners with STEM background but no quantum computing experience
